# RT031 versus Current-Service V4 on one accessibility substrate

## Result

The supplied RT031 one/two-movement pool does **not** establish a broad
accessibility replacement case against even the exact-identity subset of the
Current-Service V4 structural stop universe.

This is a bounded negative conclusion about the supplied pool. It is not a claim
that every possible redesigned network is inferior, and it selects no network.

## Comparable evidence

The standalone V4 percentages and RT031 percentages use different historical
population/walking substrates and are therefore not compared directly. This
audit instead maps Current-Service V4 to the RT028 universe only when a frozen
RT028 stop place and a V4 physical cluster share an exact official native stop
ID. No name, coordinate, fuzzy or nearest-neighbour fallback is allowed.

Eleven RT028 conventional stop places meet that rule. The resulting current
structural subset and every one of the 173,663 RT031 availability sets are then
evaluated with the same RT028 population rows, weights, graph and walk times.
Arithmetic is exact rational arithmetic; no tolerance, uncertainty band or
weighted score is used.

| Dimension | Current exact-ID structural subset | Best RT031 value anywhere in supplied pool | RT031 − current (percentage points) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Core, 5 min | 21.7376% | 23.4417% | +1.7041 |
| Core, 8 min | 38.7287% | 37.3889% | −1.3398 |
| Core, 10 min | 48.7696% | 42.9650% | −5.8046 |
| Worst municipality, 5 min | 7.0608% | 10.3202% | +3.2594 |
| Worst municipality, 8 min | 20.9192% | 19.5888% | −1.3304 |
| Worst municipality, 10 min | 34.4032% | 25.0317% | −9.3715 |

The maxima in the RT031 column may belong to different candidates. Even with
that optimistic componentwise envelope, RT031 remains below the current subset
on all four 8/10-minute total/equity dimensions. At row level:

- zero RT031 sets are no worse than the current subset on all six dimensions;
- the current subset is no worse than 173,427 of 173,663 RT031 sets on all six;
- 236 sets retain a narrow 5-minute-versus-8/10-minute trade-off, not a broad
  replacement case.

## Semantics and consequence

RT031 coverage remains potential coverage if its available stops later become
publicly served. Current-Service V4 supplies route-level current activation and
an ordinary structural stop universe, not a literal stop-level snapshot. The
eleven-stop comparator is only the exact-ID mapped subset of that universe.

Therefore this comparison is deliberately conservative toward RT031: it does
not give the current network credit for V4 stops lacking an exact RT028 identity,
while it gives every candidate full credit for all of its available stops. Even
under that asymmetry, the supplied pool cannot support a broad-access replacement
claim. Those candidates must not be promoted to final operational selection.

The next design search must expand beyond this truncated one/two-walk pool and
must bind public service events before it can return a replacement finalist.
`primary_selection_authorised=false` and
`runner_up_selection_authorised=false` remain unchanged.
