# RT-022 minimum-backbone structural diversity diagnostic V3

This note describes the certified 88-row exact minimum policy-backbone universe. It is deliberately **non-decisional**: it does not rank candidates, select service termini, infer passenger accessibility, or claim that larger structures are inferior.

## What the 88 candidates actually contain

The 88 structures use only 16 distinct stop-place identities as structural terminals. Their diversity is uneven by municipality:

- Brivio: 1 terminal option in the 88 structures, `FROZEN::100005` / Brivio - s.s. briantea, present in all 88;
- Calco: 6 terminal options;
- La Valletta Brianza: 3 terminal options;
- Olgiate Molgora: 4 terminal options, including Olgiate-Calco-Brivio FS in 28 structures;
- Santa Maria Hoè: 2 terminal options, with Alduno in 74 structures.

These frequencies are properties of the exact minimum-backbone universe, not evidence that a frequent structural terminal should become a service capolinea.

## Municipality-level feasible adjacency graph

Among the 110 admitted reciprocal structural links, only six cross-municipality adjacency families exist:

- Brivio - Calco;
- Brivio - Olgiate Molgora;
- Calco - Olgiate Molgora;
- La Valletta Brianza - Olgiate Molgora;
- La Valletta Brianza - Santa Maria Hoè;
- Olgiate Molgora - Santa Maria Hoè.

At municipality level this forms two triangles sharing Olgiate Molgora: Brivio-Calco-Olgiate and Olgiate-Santa Maria Hoè-La Valletta Brianza. This is an emergent property of the admitted reciprocal corridor corpus. It is **not** a figure-eight service requirement.

Because there is no admitted cross-family connection between the Brivio/Calco side and the Santa Maria Hoè/La Valletta side that bypasses Olgiate, Olgiate Molgora is an articulation municipality in all 88 minimum backbones. Its degree is 2 in 54 structures, 3 in 30 and 4 in 4.

## Structural compression

The 88 stop-level structures collapse to only 9 distinct municipality-level edge skeletons. The most common skeleton accounts for 24 structures, the next for 20 and the third for 16. This shows that much of the remaining multiplicity is about which existing stop identity realizes a municipality role, not only about different intermunicipal graph shapes.

## Interpretation boundary

This diagnostic cannot decide which of the 88 structures is better. RT-011 requires passenger-stop materialization, network walking/passenger routing, operational metrics on the same candidate definition and a geographic sanity gate before candidate comparison. RT-023 is responsible for the missing generic service-pattern realization bridge.

Structures with five or more links remain outside the certified minimum-edge scope and are not declared inferior.
