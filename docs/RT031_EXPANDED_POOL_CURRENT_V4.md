# RT031 expanded-pool comparison with Current-Service V4

## Conclusion

Increasing the physical-walk execution allowance from 250,000 to 1,000,000
states does not establish a broad accessibility replacement case. The enlarged
pool improves total core access, but still fails the approved strong
intermunicipal-equity safeguard at 8 and 10 minutes.

This conclusion is exact within the supplied enlarged pool and is not a proof
over all possible networks.

These are independent componentwise maxima and therefore form an optimistic

finds zero of 727,514 sets no worse than current on all six dimensions. Current
is no worse than 707,549 sets on all six; the other 19,965 are trade-offs.

## Decision consequence

No member of this enlarged pool should proceed to typed operational binding as
a broad-access replacement finalist. Further multiplication of the same bounded
search is not evidence of eventual success. The next design step must change or
broaden the candidate domain—while retaining exact road history and service-event
semantics—rather than promote a territorially weaker configuration.

Availability still does not imply public service, transfers or passenger
continuity. No timetable, dwell, recovery, demand allocation, GJT or empirical
missed-connection probability is inferred. No network is selected.
