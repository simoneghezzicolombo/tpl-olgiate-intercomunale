## Remaining integration boundary

Complete physical distances are now available for declared full route sequences.
Historical visits now have explicit supported/uncertain/no-evidence place bindings.
Production RT031 remains open. A defensible service comparison still requires:

1. Directional stop/road attachment and D184/D185 physical calibration, using the
   supported place bindings as inputs and retaining unresolved/outside-domain visits.
2. Explicit public service events, continuity, operating context and timetable;
   carrier length does not determine travel time, frequency or annual vehicle-km.
3. A prospective finite territorial candidate/service domain, equivalence and
   repetition rules, objective semantics and completeness/stopping declaration.
   The six stress scenarios do not define that domain.
4. Independent review of service semantics and this integration delta, followed
   by recomputation of access/equity and operating comparisons on the new lineage.

No budget, speed, operating days, timetable, service euro cost, PRIMARY or
RUNNER-UP was invented to fill these gaps. Existing historical operational
modules can be reused only after the new route/service inputs are explicit.
