It is a synthesis, not a new route, timetable, score, or selection. `docs/RT031_PRIOR_WORK_EVIDENCE_MAP.md` remains the navigation map; where it conflicts with later approved policy or caller corrections, the more specific later source is cited below.

## Candidate disposition supported today

### New south-Olgiate cross-frontier check

The pinned 736-profile municipal frontier has now been cross-checked against both provisional residential-area envelopes using the same RT016/RT028 walking substrate. The best 10-minute potential stop-set access among those profiles is **56.32%** in the inner area and **51.75%** in the outer area; none of the 736 reaches 80% in either. This is a bounded-domain result, not proof that no better route exists. It materially strengthens the diagnosis that the existing stop universe does not serve the caller's south-of-SS342 residential priority well. The separate hypothetical `P2V2S_0031` screen reaches approximately 85–90% only if that stop can be legally routed, safely sited, and served in useful directions. It has not been added to these 736 profiles. Exact machine output: `outputs/phase2/rt031_south_municipal_frontier_v3/screen.json`; reproducible hash-locked audit: `scripts/phase2_audit_rt031_south_municipal_frontier_v3.py`. Neither screen measures actual passenger journeys or selects a line.

1. **Figure-eight / FS-hub design family:** retain as the caller-preferred development direction. It has physical two-way witnesses and typed ordered events, but its tested stop sets under-serve Olgiate relative to some non-8 stop sets, miss the newly clarified south-Olgiate area, exceed the cited illustrative per-cycle reference, and lack a joint timetable. No single figure-eight variant is yet a defensible selected route.
2. **Arlate/Rovagnate single-line witnesses:** retain as access/route trade-off comparators. All seven in the cited pool are Pareto-nondominated; strongest total/Olgiate walk coverage does not establish fast FS access, frequency, or fulfilment of the requested itinerary. Not selected.
3. **Corrected two-movement, all-11-target witness:** retain as a development finalist for service redesign, not a final one-line recommendation. It improves total same-substrate static access, preserves the comparison target set, and can fit the cap at H60; uniform H30 exceeds the cap, while its current worst-municipality dimensions merely equal the comparator and do not meet the documented H60 exception. It is also two separate movements, not yet the caller's one recognizable line.

No candidate currently has the full bundle: one public line identity; validated ordered stop events serving a defensible subset of requested fractions; useful south-Olgiate access distinct from north San Zeno; five-municipality access/equity; chosen frequent span; explicit passenger continuity/transfers; dwell-inclusive times, fleet and annual production; and deterministic robustness. Therefore no PRIMARY or RUNNER-UP is evidence-ready.

## Near-term provisional recommendation: what can and cannot be said

A **conditional design recommendation** is supportable: advance one recognizable public-facing service concept centred on Olgiate FS, preserve the west/east figure-eight as the preferred topology hypothesis for comparison, and explicitly test a shorter or differentiated-frequency version against the corrected two-movement and non-8 comparators. Treat south-Olgiate boarding/access as a priority gap with Via Aldo Moro only a field-check hypothesis. Carry locality service, stop access, span/headway, two-direction FS journeys, passenger continuity/transfers, vehicle blocks, annual km and deterministic robustness together as the comparison record. This follows the caller's stated preferences and approved policy; it does not claim that a figure-eight wins.

That provisional direction does **not** require spatialized OD, demand-weighted GJT, or empirical missed-connection probabilities: the approved V3 pathway explicitly does not require them, provided none are claimed and certified accessibility/equity plus deterministic engineering metrics are used. It also does not require choosing a new `decision_budget_km` or `uncertainty_band_min`: the approved policy already sets the 111,419 km/year cap and expressly sets no global scalar uncertainty band. But a **final candidate ranking or PRIMARY/RUNNER-UP selection is not supportable** without the remaining service/timetable and south-area stop evidence, candidate-level gate results on a common finalized lineage, and whatever explicit budget-envelope/finalizer invocation the contract requires. `network_selected=false`, `primary_selection_authorised=false`, and `runner_up_selection_authorised=false` remain the correct state.
