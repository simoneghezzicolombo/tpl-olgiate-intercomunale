## Controlled adversarial result

Two controlled candidates both place 100% of population within 15 walking minutes, but the near-service case has population-weighted mean walking time 4.25 min and P90 5 min, while the far-service case has mean 12.5 min and P90 14 min. The contract therefore prevents a single broad catchment threshold from hiding actual walking burden.

The controlled Pareto test preserves both a lower-km candidate and a better-access candidate when neither dominates the other. A third candidate that is strictly worse on every declared dimension is removed.

A missing generic policy group fails the territorial guard. An unserved generic service area remains an explicit diagnostic with marginal-service fields and does not become a forced waypoint.

## Non-claims

This PASS does not rank or recommend any territorial network. It does not consume the evolving stop inventory. Real evaluation remains blocked until corrected passenger-stop materialization and passenger-routing evidence are available.
