# RT031 service convergence screen — non-decisional

**Status: 24 September 2026.** This screen collects operational evidence that can narrow the next service-design comparison. It does not choose a service, fill Decision Contract fields, or turn candidate counts into votes. The 736 municipal-frontier profiles are valid members of a bounded Pareto family; the one-line, directional and timetable declarations are candidate designs, not observed operations.

## What is genuinely comparable

The most useful resource-equivalent service policy tested is 20 departures per day over 18 hours: two H30 peak hours and sixteen H60-only hours. It has the same daily departures as the 12/14/16-hour alternatives, with more span and fewer H30 hours. In the complete fleet screen for all 736 within-reference-cap profiles, every profile requires at most three vehicles across the 27 deterministic running/dwell/recovery cases. Fleet maxima are 19 profiles at one vehicle, 568 at two and 149 at three. Every profile retaining 7–10 of the 11 exact current-stop identities requires three vehicles. Each 12/14/16-hour alternative has 108 four-vehicle profiles; the remaining 628 are at most three. This makes 18h a credible *policy family for comparison*, not a selected span or a three-vehicle guarantee for real operation. Annual production remains conditional on 20 departures/day × 260 days. The later human-approved policy records a 111,419 bus-km/year hard cap, while `decision_budget_km` for the finalizer remains separately caller-declared.

The service fleet evidence is pinned by the [fleet audit](../outputs/phase2/rt031_municipal_frontier_fleet_screen_v4/municipal_frontier_fleet_screen_v4_audit.json): run `35587456801`, artifact `10632938711`, ZIP SHA256 `1dda77b013bf7cf0d95e1633a9128e8f5840af1f0fb44e54d48d387e444af627`, result SHA256 `05290996d50a5c6608dd5712e594302034db2b01b76a8b2525c54b5487442624`.

For those 736 candidates, the complete H30-peak/H60-base 18h phase surface evaluates 30 clock phases and all 27 engineering cases: 22,080 candidate/phase contexts, 596,160 realisations. Per-candidate Pareto filtering retains 14,395 contexts (5–26 phases per line) and removes none of the 736 lines. Thus the phase calculation identifies viable phase alternatives inside each line, but cannot discriminate among the lines. The phase audit explicitly says `pareto_frontier_computed=false` globally. There is no combined cross-line operational Pareto ranking, and no weighted or OD-based service score. Source: [18h phase audit](../outputs/phase2/rt031_full_municipal_18h_phase_surface_v4/full_municipal_18h_phase_surface_v4_audit.json), run `35588461021`, artifact `10633402248`, ZIP SHA256 `1eb77d06a3fb0dda5d0b92217cbe1cd386ec59d39ae69d5280abbb43f16180e7`, result SHA256 `4be12d21db6bf63cd87937df9baafaeeabbce7b86b160adf6dee0c7ab5dd1cfd`.

Within-line phase quality is conditional on the frozen official S8 event list and three assumed transfer-friction profiles. Quality is kept directional (rail-to-bus and bus-to-rail, Milano and Lecco) and profile-specific, with combined hub maximum gap as another axis. For the separate two-movement H60/16h hub-witness design, 2,236 of 3,600 phase pairs remain non-dominated; all 60 rotations with an exact 30-minute combined hub gap also remain non-dominated. That result is direct evidence that phase preference cannot be inferred from one “best” rotation. The [hub-phase audit](../outputs/phase2/rt031_hub_phase_frontier_v3/rt031_hub_phase_frontier_v3_audit.json) is a clock-phase diagnostic, not a daily timetable; transfer profiles are assumptions, and deterministic misses are not empirical probabilities.

## Directional FS journey evidence and gaps

The hub-split patterns prove a physical and typed topology that goes FS → west lobe → FS → east lobe → FS (and reverse), with one declared public line identity per directional pattern. That supports testing the intended one-line concept. It is not evidence that a passenger can use both directions on a common schedule: the typed declaration terminates passenger continuity at the cycle seam and does not combine the two directional alternatives into an hourly service. Nor does a stop set or ten-minute catchment provide minutes from a specific locality to FS.

For the 736 one-line profiles, ordered public event occurrences are bound, and the phase surface checks deterministic bus/rail interactions against the frozen S8 event set. Yet this is not an OD journey matrix: municipality OD has not been spatially downscaled, no population-to-stop or passenger weights are used for transfer quality, and the published complete-surface audit does not produce direct/indirect locality-to-FS journey times in each direction. “Potential walking access,” “ordered event present,” and “rail connection quality under a phase assumption” are separate claims. A credible next comparison needs per-locality, per-direction summaries for all five municipalities with ordered origin stop→FS elapsed time, any wait and onboard continuity stated explicitly, as well as the direction-specific rail transfer metrics already available in the phase model.

## Decision-ready limits

- Supported: exact identity retention (as a Pareto preference), route cycle distance, separate municipality walking-access measures, typed ordered events, candidate-pattern continuity statements, deterministic fleet envelopes under the inherited 27-case grid, and phase-specific rail-transfer quality on the stated timetable assumptions.
- Missing or not certified: complete territorial candidate domain; a selected fleet/span/frequency pattern; observed dwell/runtime/reliability; actual safe stops in the corrected south-Olgiate residential area; explicit direct and reverse locality→FS journeys for every municipality; actual jointly operated two-direction schedule for the figure-eight; operational blocks/turns and layover evidence; passenger-weighted GJT or empirically calibrated missed-transfer probability.
- The 111,419 km/year cap is human-approved in `config/phase2_final_policy_contract_v3.json`; 20 departures/day and 260 service days are still a comparison scenario, not a selected operating plan. Candidate pools are resource-truncated or bounded; frontier membership means non-dominated only on named axes within the named pool. The separate finalizer input `decision_budget_km` must not be filled by inference.

Accordingly, the evidence supports carrying at least three service-design families into a caller-led comparison: (1) the 18h H30-peak/H60-base members of the 736 municipal frontier; (2) the Brivio + Santa Maria typed corridor family as a territorial coverage benchmark, conditional on additional phase/fleet binding for those exact lines; and (3) the FS-hinged figure-eight variants as a structural and directional alternative, with its weak Olgiate coverage and joint-timetable gap exposed. It does not support a primary/runner-up designation or an operating timetable.
